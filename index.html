About Midox

30 years of industrial valve expertise

Midox Brewery Components s.r.o. was founded in 1993 in Nezamyslice, Czech Republic.

Today we combine three competencies: distribution, own manufacturing, and technical consultancy.

Authorised Czech representative for ETATRON (Italy) dosing pumps.

Company history

1993

Founded

Company established. First products: KOMO steam traps and pressure-reducing valves.
